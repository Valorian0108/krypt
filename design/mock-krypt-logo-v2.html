What this is not

No coin. No chain. No key. No lock. No triangle. No pyramid. No glowing circle. Nothing that reads as cryptocurrency. The seal is a vault door, not a token.

3. Motion state

Seal pulses when a purchase waits for your approval

Behavior

Idle: seal rests against the inner ring edge.
Pending: seal drifts up and left, ring pulses slightly.
Approved: seal breaks away from the ring entirely (handled in app code, not CSS).
CSS animation only. No video. No glassmorphism.
